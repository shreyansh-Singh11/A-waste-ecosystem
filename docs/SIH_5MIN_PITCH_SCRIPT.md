## Jury Defense Cheat Sheet (Answering the Top 6 Tough Questions)

### Q1: *"What if a citizen uploads a fake or blurry image to game the AI credits?"*
> **Answer:** *"CirculaSync enforces a dual-gate architecture. If Gemini AI confidence is below 75%, it is automatically tagged 'Pending Physical Verification'. Furthermore, reward coins remain locked in escrow until the physical intake scan at the recycler's SSQI conveyor confirms weight and model match. No payout occurs on unverified optical scans."*

### Q2: *"How do you prevent registered recyclers from forming a cartel in your reverse auction?"*
> **Answer:** *"Our reverse auction operates with blind bidding decrements and reserve floors calibrated dynamically to live London Metal Exchange (LME) spot commodity rates for copper, aluminum, and gold. Bids below statutory recovery margins are automatically rejected, and escrow deposits prevent shill bids."*

### Q3: *"How does your Eulerian routing mathematically guarantee zero skipped streets?"*
> **Answer:** *"Traditional TSP solves point-to-point visits, leaving street segments unvisited. We model the city road network as an undirected multigraph and solve the Chinese Postman Problem by adding minimum-weight matching paths between odd-degree vertices, transforming the graph into a Eulerian circuit where every edge (street) is traversed at least once with minimal redundant transit."*

### Q4: *"Can a medical contractor dump hazardous needles into regular municipal landfills?"*
> **Answer:** *"No. Biomedical waste is barred from municipal weighbridges. Municipal RFID gates check waste category tags; if a medical barcode is detected, the gate rejects tare capture and immediately generates an automated Grade-1 Bio-Hazard Incident alert on the State Pollution Control Board radar."*

### Q5: *"Can an electronics brand buy the same recycling certificate twice or forge certificates?"*
> **Answer:** *"Impossible by design. Every EPR certificate is minted with a SHA-256 cryptographic hash derived from the physical recycler's weighbridge log, batch ID, and timestamp. The moment a brand completes purchase, the certificate status flips permanently to 'RETIRED' in the ledger, preventing any double-counting or resale."*

### Q6: *"Is this system feasible for tier-3 towns or rural gram panchayats with poor internet?"*
> **Answer:** *"Yes. CirculaSync is built as an offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA). Sanitation drivers and collectors can record QR scans, weighbridge gross/tare, and GPS coordinates locally in encrypted IndexedDB. Once connectivity is restored, the queue syncs with the central ledger using conflict-free timestamp ordering."*
