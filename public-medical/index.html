2. Dedicated GPS Transport Dispatch

Bio-medical waste must be transported in leak-proof, authorized vehicles only

BMW Transport Safety Protocol

  • Zero Mixing: Under no circumstances can bio-medical waste be combined with municipal trash.
  • Transit Window: Waste must reach Common Bio-medical Waste Treatment Facility (CBWTF) within 48 hours.
  • Spill Kits Onboard: Vehicle must carry sodium hypochlorite solution, PPE, and absorbent pads.

3. CBWTF Facility Weighbridge Arrival

Weighbridge gross/tare reconciliation against hospital-declared weight
Reconciliation Verification:

The system automatically calculates Net Weight = Gross − Tare and flags any variance exceeding ±5% from the hospital manifest to prevent illicit diversion of recyclable plastic (IV tubes/syringes).
